About PlaylistPro

Automatic YouTube Watch Later playlist organization

What is PlaylistPro?

PlaylistPro automatically organizes your YouTube Watch Later playlist based on your preferences. It prioritizes videos from your favorite creators, groups series together, and sorts by duration so you always know what to watch next.

How It Works

Configure

Set up priority creators, keyphrases, and sequential viewing preferences

Sort

Click the sort button to reorganize your Watch Later playlist

Watch

Enjoy a perfectly organized playlist tailored to your preferences

Sorting Priority

  1. Priority Videos - Videos from creators or with keyphrases you've marked as high priority
  2. Sequential Creators - Videos from creators whose content should be watched in order (by publish date)
  3. Series - Videos detected as part of a series, naturally sorted
  4. Remaining Videos - Sorted by duration (shortest first), then by publish date
  5. Follow-ups - Videos marked as follow-ups are positioned after their predecessors

API Access

You can trigger sorting programmatically:

Endpoint Method Description
/api/sort GET Synchronous sort (blocks until complete)
/api/sort POST Start async sort, returns job_id
/api/sort/status/{job_id} GET Check async job status
/api/sort/stream GET SSE stream for real-time progress

Built with Flask, SQLAlchemy, and the YouTube Data API
